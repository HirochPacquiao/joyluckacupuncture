What we treat

Six areas we see most often

Treatment is built around your history, not a protocol off a chart. Most people come in for one of these — though plenty arrive with something else entirely, and that's fine too.

Chronic and acute pain

Back, neck, shoulder, joint, and nerve pain. Needling at specific points modulates the pain response, reduces inflammation, and improves circulation to the area — without adding another medication.

Stress and anxiety

Acupuncture helps regulate the autonomic nervous system and lower circulating cortisol. Many people describe the sessions themselves as the most rested they've felt all week.

Fertility and reproductive health

Improved pelvic blood flow and hormone regulation support both natural conception and IVF or IUI cycles. We coordinate timing around your clinic's protocol.

Digestive health

Bloating, constipation, reflux, and IBS-type symptoms. Treatment targets gastrointestinal motility and inflammation to improve digestion and nutrient absorption.

Immune support

For people who catch everything going around, or who are recovering slowly. Treatment supports circulation and lymphatic drainage to help the body do its own work.

General wellness

Sleep, energy, and the vague run-down feeling that doesn't have a name yet. Regular sessions to keep things steady rather than waiting for something to go wrong.

Your first visit

What actually happens

If you've never had acupuncture, the unknown is usually the thing holding you back. Here's the whole ninety minutes.

01 — Intake

We talk first

Your symptoms, history, sleep, digestion, stress, medications. Around 20 minutes, and it shapes everything that follows.

02 — Diagnosis

Pulse and tongue

Two traditional diagnostic readings that tell us how your system is currently running, alongside what you've described.

03 — Treatment

Needles, then rest

Hair-thin, single-use, sterile. Most people feel a dull heaviness at most. You rest with them in place for 20–30 minutes.

04 — Plan

Where to go next

An honest read on how many sessions your condition typically needs, plus anything to change at home. No packages pushed on you.
